recipe for cooking omlette(ol)

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Whisk the eggs until well blended.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add butter.
  4. Pour the whisked eggs into the skillet.
  5. Cook until the edges start to set, then gently lift them with a spatula.
  6. Add fillings like cheese, vegetables, or meats if desired.
  7. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute.
  8. Slide the omelette onto a plate and serve hot.
